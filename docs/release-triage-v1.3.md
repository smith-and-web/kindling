against `main` at `5998937` (including PR #340). No issue discussion was present.

and the risk of broad compatibility changes shortly before a release. Labels have
not been changed. Deferred means **v1.3.1 follow-up**, not resolved or unimportant.

## User-visible recovery behavior

- A Part deletion that encounters a locked chapter or scene deletes nothing and
  displays the error. Unlock the protected content before retrying.
- Selective sync preserves metadata that was not selected; protected content stays
  protected even if it was locked after preview.
- If a mode change reports an unsaved draft, use Find and Replace's recovery flow
  to retry or explicitly discard it, then switch modes again.
- When author settings cannot load, Settings → Author & Contact offers **Reset
  author details**. It keeps the original file as `settings-recovery-<id>.json`
  beside `settings.json`. Re-enter and save author details before exporting.
- Scrivener bundles with escaping content paths are rejected. Missing, unreadable, or legacy documents without a UUID retain the existing empty-document fallback.
